TL;DR: SLP-Bench是一个用于评估音频语言模型（ALM）在临床语音中推理链中每个声学声明的基准，涵盖5个临床标注数据集和25个感知维度，评估声明的事实性、重采样稳定性和改写一致性。在10个零样本推理模型中，声明平衡准确率在0.52-0.58之间；通过GRPO对7B ALM进行微调，声明平衡准确率从0.54提升至0.60，平均kappa从0.39提升至0.61，同时保持每个维度问题的答案准确性。

Motivation: 现有的音频语言模型（ALM）能够通过语音回答临床问题，且表现优于基于手工声学特征的基线方法。推理型ALM会在预测前生成关于说话者声音属性的推理链，但这些声称需要与临床医生的感知评级进行核对。然而，现有的评估仅关注最终答案，未能评估推理链中每个声学声明的准确性。

Method: 提出了SLP-Bench基准，用于评估推理链中的每个声学声明。该基准覆盖5个临床标注数据集和25个感知维度，从事实性、重采样稳定性和改写一致性三个维度评估声明。在10个开源推理ALM上进行零样本评估，并使用GRPO对7B ALM进行基于临床医生一致奖励的微调。

Abstract: Audio language models (ALMs) can answer clinical questions from speech, outperforming baselines built on handcrafted acoustic features. Reasoning ALMs generate a chain of thought that refers to properties of the speaker's voice before predicting. Each statement the model makes about the voice needs to be checked against clinicians' perceptual ratings of the same recording. On clinical speech, existing evaluations score only the final answer. We introduce Speech Language Pathologist Bench (SLP-Bench), a benchmark grading each acoustic claim in the chain of thought against clinicians' perceptual ratings of the same recording. It covers five clinician-rated datasets and 25 perceptual dimensions, and assesses claims for factuality, stability under resampling, and consistency across reworded and different questions. Across ten open-weight reasoning ALMs evaluated zero-shot, claim balanced accuracy ranges from 0.52 to 0.58, claims change under resampling and rewording, and the most consistent models are not the most factual. Fine-tuning a 7B ALM with GRPO on a reward for claims that agree with clinicians raises its claim balanced accuracy from 0.54 to 0.60 and its mean kappa from 0.39 to 0.61, while preserving answer accuracy on per-dimension questions. SLP-Bench is available upon request, subject to approval by the providers of each dataset.

### [10] [Windchime: an Audiovisual Installation with Audio-Language Model implementations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26759)
*Trent Eriksen,Celeste Betancur Gutierrez*

Main category: cs.MM

TL;DR: Windchime是一个通过语音检索音乐和交互动画的视听装置：本地ASR转写语音，音频语言模型嵌入查询，从406个音频片段中检索生成声音景观，同时语音输入检索Three.js动画场景并通过monome硬件实现触觉控制，将能动性分布在艺术家、语言模型和参观者三方。

Abstract: Windchime is an audiovisual installation in which spoken language retrieves music and interactive animations from an artist authored corpus. A local Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) model transcribes each prompt while an audio-language model (ALM) embeds it and retrieval over 406 audio stems returns a soundscape with live coded Strudel fragments that layer into audio output. Simultaneously each spoken input results in retrieval of interactive Three.js animation scenes each containing unique tactile control via monome grid and arc hardware. Windchime distributes agency in three distinct directions; across the artist who authored the possible outputs, the ALM that influences what areas of the corpus can be retrieved, and the visitor whose spoken inputs result in multimodal embodied interaction.
