TL;DR: 提出一种基于卡尔曼滤波的修正方法，通过少量人工审核的窗口估计预测误差的偏移和漂移，在线修正所有未审核窗口的连续情感识别结果，无需改变预测模型本身。

Motivation: 连续情感识别的预测常与人工评分存在偏差，且实时评分耗时昂贵。少量人工审核虽有帮助，但现有方法（拒绝或自适应）无法直接利用审核结果修正未审核窗口。

Method: 采用卡尔曼滤波修正，将预测器误差建模为每段偏移加缓慢漂移，从已审核窗口估计该误差，并在线修正所有未审核窗口，预测器本身保持不变。

Result: 在MSP-Conversation数据集上，与四种更简单的修正方法相比，在48组成对比较中全部胜出；在审核停止后仍能持续修正每段；对未修正的预测，能告知用户应接管哪些窗口。以20%窗口被审核时，完成的情感轨迹更接近评分者。

Conclusion: 所提卡尔曼滤波修正方法有效提升了连续情感识别的准确性，可同时服务于预测修正和标注加速两个任务，具有较好的实用价值。

Abstract: Continuous emotion recognition tracks how emotion changes over time, but its predictions often deviate from human ratings, and the traces it is trained on are rated in real time by several annotators, which is slow and costly. A few human reviews could help with both, but rejection only decides which predictions to hand over and adaptation adjusts the model or its outputs; neither uses reviews to correct unreviewed windows directly. We propose a Kalman-filter correction that models the predictor's error as a per-part offset plus a slow drift, estimates it from the reviewed windows and corrects every unreviewed window online, leaving the predictor unchanged. On MSP-Conversation, it beats four simpler corrections in all 48 paired comparisons, keeps correcting each part after reviewing stops and, for uncorrected predictions, tells a person which windows to take over. The same correction serves annotation: with 20% of windows reviewed, the completed trace comes closer to the raters.
