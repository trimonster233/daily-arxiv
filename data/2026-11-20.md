### [1] [Blind tests of a simple-body model of submarine target strength on open benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25977)
*Sunil Tyagi*

Main category: eess.AS

TL;DR: 研究用三个公开基准检验简化声学模型。它能近似部分测量结果，但在局部共振等条件下误差明显。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 常用数值模型计算开销较高，作者希望了解简化模型在哪些条件下仍能给出可靠的近似。

Method: 作者将简化模型的预测与公开数值解和湖试数据进行对照，其中两项测试在查阅基准结果前固定了预测。

Result: 部分工况下计算值与基准吻合，但在共振等条件下出现明显偏差。

Conclusion: 这些基准只验证了部分声学效应，尚不足以证明模型能够描述完整的回波特性。

Abstract: Finite-element and boundary-element models of a submarine's sonar echo need a mesh and a solver, and their cost grows quickly with frequency. SSR-Sim takes an analytical route. The hull is cut into a few simple bodies, the echo of each is a closed-form result of geometrical acoustics or of the Kirchhoff approximation, the surface enters through a plane-wave reflection coefficient, and the echoes add in energy. This paper describes the method, including the reduction of a hull and its sail to simple bodies, and tests it on three open cases. (1) The reflection coefficient of a 50 mm rubber tile on steel plating, computed with finite elements by Yang et al., is reproduced to 0.02 rms or better at incidence angles from 0° to 75° once the layer is allowed to refract. (2) For the rigid full-scale BeTSSi submarine at 1 kHz, a prediction fixed before the reference was opened agrees with the boundary-element results of Venås and Kvamsdal to within 0.7 dB in every aspect sector from the bow to 150°; in the stern sector it is 5 dB high. (3) For a 1:15 BeTSSi model with 10 mm tiles measured in a lake, a second blind prediction is within -3.1, +1.4 and -0.5 dB of the measured level over aspects 0-120° at 5, 15 and 20 kHz, against -3.1, +3.1 and +3.5 dB for the Kirchhoff-finite-element computation published with the measurement. At 10 kHz, on a resonance of the tile, the prediction is 6.9 dB low, and in the bow sector it is 7 dB or more low at every frequency. The tests cover the specular component and the coating model only. The failures show what such a model lacks: edge diffraction and the returns that set the off-beam floor of a real model.

### [2] [UMelCodec: An Ultra-Low-Bitrate and Lightweight Mel-Domain Neural Speech Codec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25569)
*Hui-Peng Du,Yang Ai,Zhen-Hua Ling*

Main category: eess.AS

TL;DR: UMelCodec 以 325 比特/秒编码语音，在 LibriTTS 上兼顾了重建质量和实时推理速度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 极低码率使语音的可懂度、自然度和细节难以同时保留，引入语义编码又可能增加模型复杂度。

Method: 模型先在梅尔频谱域用单码本向量量化压缩语音，再用基于修正离散余弦变换的轻量声码器重建波形。

Result: 在 LibriTTS 上，多数客观重建指标优于对照方法，主观自然度相近。模型有 1967 万参数，在 CPU 和 GPU 上均可实时推理。

Conclusion: 在该数据集上，纯声学表示也能以很低的码率实现实时语音编解码。

Abstract: Ultra-low-bitrate speech coding is crucial for bandwidth-constrained communication and compact storage, but preserving intelligibility, naturalness, and fine-grained acoustic details at bitrates of only a few hundred bps remains challenging. Existing purely acoustic codecs generally operate at relatively high bitrates, whereas incorporating semantic representations can reduce the bitrate, but at the cost of increased complexity. Therefore, we propose UMelCodec, a purely acoustic neural speech codec operating in the mel domain at an ultra-low bitrate of 325 bps with a lightweight architecture. UMelCodec consists of two stages. In the first mel-domain coding stage, alias-free Snake-ConvNeXt (AF-SC) encoder-decoder backbones model temporal and harmonic structures, a factorized single-codebook vector quantizer (VQ) enables ultra-low-bitrate quantization, and a multi-scale mel-spectrogram discriminator (MSMD) improves mel-spectrogram reconstruction. In the second waveform synthesis stage, a lightweight modified discrete cosine transform (MDCT)-based neural vocoder predicts the MDCT spectrum from the reconstructed mel spectrogram and recovers the waveform through inverse MDCT. Experiments on LibriTTS dataset demonstrate that UMelCodec outperforms strong baselines across most objective reconstruction metrics while maintaining competitive subjective naturalness. In terms of model complexity and inference efficiency, UMelCodec has only 19.67 M parameters, requires 1.93 G MACs, and supports real-time inference on both GPU and CPU platforms.

</details>


### [3] [Joint Language Modeling for In-the-Wild Speech-Scene Generation with Distinct Token Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25503)
*Semin Kim,Ju Yeon Kang,Sunghwan Ahn,Seonuk Kim,Nam Soo Kim*

Main category: eess.AS

TL;DR: 模型从混合录音中分开表示语音和环境声，再交错生成两者，以提升语音可懂度和场景一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 语音和环境声的特性不同，而独立录音再混合又难以保留真实场景中两者的关联。

Method: 音频编解码器从混合录音中提取时间对齐的两路离散表示，预训练语言模型根据场景描述和台词交错预测两路序列。

Result: 与已有语音场景生成方法相比，作者报告了语音可懂度、场景相关性和音质的提升，使用真实录音适配后进一步改善。

Conclusion: 分别表示声源、联合建模上下文，使模型能够从没有独立音轨的真实录音中学习。

Abstract: Speech-scene generation aims to synthesize intelligible target speech together with diverse environmental sounds as a coherent acoustic scene. The task is considered challenging in two respects. Speech and environmental audio differ substantially in their semantic and acoustic characteristics, yet existing approaches typically model the complete scene using a single mixed acoustic representation. At the same time, the two sounds are closely related in real-world speech scenes, whereas conventional approaches usually rely on simulated scenes mixed from independently recorded sources. We propose a large language model (LLM)-based framework for joint modeling of distinct speech and environment token streams. A source-aware neural audio codec extracts temporally aligned speech and environment tokens from mixed audio, and a pretrained LLM generates both token streams in an interleaved fashion conditioned on the natural-language scene description and speech content. This formulation preserves source-specific representations while modeling the token streams within a shared generative context. Since both streams are extracted directly from mixed recordings, the model can also be adapted to in-the-wild speech scenes without isolated sources. Our experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework improves speech intelligibility, scene relevance, and audio quality over existing speech-scene generation techniques, with consistent gains from real-world adaptation. We further investigate the effects of distinct token representations and interleaved generation, and analyze whether the environmental sound remains stable while speech is active and how the two streams interact.

</details>

### [4] [ZoionNET: Low-Power Animal Intrusion Detection Using an Acoustic Deep-Learning Classifier for a Cellular Remote-Sensing System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26112)
*Prasanna Vijay,Adarsh Gupta,Vansh Panwar,Vatsalya Singh,Shubham Singhal,Anubhav Saha,Teena Sharma*

Main category: cs.SD

TL;DR: ZoionNET 是面向林区边缘社区的电池供电声学监测节点，能识别动物并通过蜂窝网络发送预警。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 常开的小模型先筛查声音，疑似有动物时再启动四分类模型。设备另配振动传感器，并通过蜂窝网络向网页和护林员发送结果。

Result: 两个模型在留出音频上的准确率分别为 97.4% 和 94.6%，单节点部件成本为 133.57 美元。

Abstract: Crop raiding and village intrusion by elephants and large felids impose a recurring cost on communities along forest boundaries in southern India, and existing camera-based monitoring has proven difficult to sustain on the power budgets available at such sites. We present ZoionNET, a battery-powered sensing node that detects animal intrusion acoustically rather than visually. Audio from an onboard PDM microphone is sampled at 8 kHz, converted to mel-spectrograms on-device, and classified on an Arm Ethos-U55 microNPU by two int8-quantised YOLOv8 models arranged as a two-state machine: a lightweight always-on model runs in a dormant state and gates a four-class species classifier through exponential-moving-average thresholds, so the heavier model runs only when an intrusion is likely. A digital vibration sensor supplies a complementary seismic channel. Inferences leave the node as JSON over a cellular Notecard, reaching a web dashboard and SMS alerts to forest rangers without any local gateway. The two models reach reported accuracies of 0.974 and 0.946 on held-out audio, and a complete node costs USD 133.57 in parts. We report the system as built and deployed, and identify explicitly what has not yet been measured, most notably the energy behaviour that motivates the two-state design.

### [5] [When Sounds Turn Heads: Recovering Event Timing That Audio LLMs Fail to Say](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25960)
*Jongbhin Woo,Jaewook Jeong,Joon Son Chung*

Main category: cs.SD

TL;DR: 音频大模型的部分注意力头能够定位目标声音。用这些信号调整解码，无需训练即可改善回答中的时间戳。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 模型给出的时间区间常与声音不符，作者希望区分声音定位能力与输出时间戳的能力。

Method: 研究识别会随查询及声音位置移动的注意力头，保留模型对事件是否存在的判断，再用这些头提供的时间信息重排候选时间戳。

Result: 该方法改善了三个音频大模型的时间戳。在模型训练后发布的录音上，平均交并比从 0.07–0.36 提高到 0.48–0.56。

Conclusion: 时间戳错误有一部分发生在答案生成阶段，模型内部已有的时间信息可用于纠正这些错误。

Abstract: Audio LLMs can know when a queried sound occurs but fail to say it. We find a few attention heads that track the queried sound: they shift when the query changes, follow the sound when it moves, and removing or blocking them often hurts the model's timestamps. Yet the answer does not express what they track. Intervals read directly from these heads outperform the model's original answer and recover events that its timestamps miss. To bring this timing into the answer, we introduce head-guided decoding, which needs no training. It keeps the model's decision on whether the sound is present and rescores its candidate timestamps with a prior from these heads. Heads selected on development data transfer unchanged to new recordings and free-text queries, where head-guided decoding improves the timestamps of three audio LLMs, scores above their head readout on every benchmark, and, in most settings, outperforms random-head guidance. On recordings released after the models were trained, head-guided decoding raises mIoU from 0.07-0.36 to 0.48-0.56. These results suggest that poor temporal answers in audio LLMs partly reflect a failure to say rather than to hear, and that the model's own attention can close part of this gap.

</details>


### [6] [From Attention to Semantics: Detecting and Mitigating Sound Event Hallucinations in Large Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25789)
*Tianyi Sun,Minghao Lv,Chunyang Yu,Shenyue Wang,Mengyue Wu*

Main category: cs.SD

TL;DR: 研究通过检查模型关注的音频片段是否在语义上支持生成内容，识别并减少音频大模型虚构声音事件的现象。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对真实和虚构事件，模型分配的音频注意力总量可能相近，仅观察总量难以判断事件是否真实。

Method: 作者改造 LatentLens，检索邻近的上下文化文本表示，用它评估语义支持度，再对不同音频区域施加区别化掩蔽，保护有证据支持的内容。

Result: 在三个音频语言模型和两个数据集上，作者报告该方法同时提升了虚构事件的检测效果与抑制效果。

Conclusion: 关注位置及其语义信息，为无需训练地减少声音事件幻觉提供了依据。

Abstract: Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) excel at diverse audio tasks, yet remain prone to hallucinating sound events absent from the input. Prior work links such hallucinations to insufficient audio attention. However, we find that real and hallucinated events can receive comparable audio attention mass while attending to different audio positions. To examine whether the attended representations support the generated events, we adapt LatentLens to retrieve neighboring contextualized text representations. The retrieved contexts reveal semantic support for real events, but a semantic mismatch between hallucinated events and their attended audio representations. Directly masking the corresponding audio regions can suppress hallucinated events at the cost of real content. We are motivated by these findings to introduce an explainable and training-free detect-then-mitigate framework for effectively hallucination detection and mitigation that aggregates semantic support and applies differential audio masking to protect regions associated with events predicted to be supported. Extensive experiments on three LALMs and two datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves hallucination detection and hallucination mitigation performance.

</details>
